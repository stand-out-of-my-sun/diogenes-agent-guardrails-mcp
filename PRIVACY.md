# Privacy Policy — agent-guardrails

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

The `agent-guardrails` Model Context Protocol (MCP) server is an independent, stateless microservice package part of the Diogenes AI Gateway ecosystem.

## 1. Zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- No user accounts, registration, email collection, or KYC are required.
- No analytics or tracking scripts are deployed.

## 2. Stateless Execution
- Tool invocations are processed in stateless, ephemeral isolate environments.
- Zero disk logging or persistence of prompts, payloads, or outputs.
- No inputs or outputs are used to train AI models.

## 3. Cryptographic Settlement
- Payments (where applicable) are processed exclusively through decentralized cryptographic rails (L402 Lightning Network, Solana x402, Base MPP). No payment card or banking information is handled.
